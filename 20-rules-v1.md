# Aiming for the Stars — Rulebook v1

*Earth is gone. The Houses remember. Now they argue about who inherits the sky.*

---

## 1. Welcome, Commander

You lead one of six Houses that escaped the death of Earth. Each House has its own
soul — its strengths, its flaws, and its own idea of what survival means.

You will claim sectors of the sector-map, raise colonies on new worlds, harvest the
remains of the old one, and fight the Houses that stand in your way. All the while,
**Earth's Last Breath** — the doom track at the table's heart — runs out.

When Earth finally falls, the House with the greatest foothold among the stars wins.
Or — if you secretly complete your Exodus objective — you win *right then*, and the
future is yours.

**You need:** 60–90 minutes · 3–6 players · a table · a printer that makes dreams real.

## 3. How You Win

**Two ways:**

1. **Exodus (instant win).** Each player starts with a secret Exodus objective.
   The moment you fulfill it at the end of a round, the game ends immediately and
   you win. Tell no one. Or hint. That's allowed.

2. **Highest score when Earth falls.** When the doom track ends, score points:
   | Source | Points |
   |--------|--------|
   | Each Colony Dome | 3 |
   | Each 3 claimed sectors | 1 |
   | Most claimed sectors (if strictly alone in the lead) | 2 |
   | Each bold dilemma choice you made | 1 |
   | Each full 5 points of morale | 1 |
   | Concord only: each 4 tolls collected | 1 |
   Ties: higher morale wins; then more colonies; then share the stars.

## 4. Setup

1. Build the map: take the tiles for your player count (§16), stack them face-down
   by type, and follow the layout diagram for your count. Match wormhole sockets
   on neighboring tiles.
2. Each player: take a House sheet, its matching tokens, 1 Morale dial (**set to 4**),
   and 1 random **Exodus card** (keep secret!).
3. Place your House's home sector per the diagram: put 1 **Colony Dome** there,
   your starting Frigates (usually 2), and 1 claim marker.
4. Starting resources: **3 Alloy, 3 Fuel, 1 Data.** Draw **2 Tactical Doctrine cards.**
5. Shuffle the Drift deck. Set the Earth dial to segment 0.
6. The player who most recently gazed at the stars takes the First Player token.

*House exceptions — see your House sheet: Sorvath starts with +3 Alloy; Volkrath with
3 Frigates; Ellarien places 1 extra Colony Dome; the Choir starts with 4 cards;
the Concord with +3 Fuel; the Ashkari scatter their fleets and start with +1 Fuel.*

---

## 5. The Round

Each round has three steps:

1. **Upkeep** (do together):
   - Advance the doom track: **1 segment** in rounds 1–4, **2 segments** from round 5.
     If it reaches the end, the game is over — score now!
   - **Collect income** (§7).
   - If the doom track crossed a **dilemma icon** (every 3rd segment), each player
     resolves 1 dilemma card (§11).
   - From round 2: flip the top card of the **Drift deck** and resolve it (§10).
   - Reset all Harvest tokens.
2. **House Turns:** players take turns in **initiative order: lowest morale first!**
   (Ties: lowest score first; then clockwise.) On your turn, take **2 actions** (§6).
3. **End of round:** if you have more claimed sectors than units in your whole fleet,
   **lose 1 morale** (overextension). Pass the First Player token.

---

## 6. Your Turn: 2 Actions

Choose any combination of these, repeats allowed:

### MOVE (cost: 1 Fuel, +1 extra for House Sorvath)
Move **one fleet** — all your Frigates and Outposts in one sector — to a sector up to
your move range away (usually 1). You may **not pass through** sectors containing
enemy Frigates.
- If the destination has enemy Frigates: **a battle begins!** (§8)
- If the destination has a wormhole socket, you may instead exit at the matching
  socket anywhere on the map (same cost; free for the Concord with Tier 1).

### CLAIM (cost: 1 Fuel)
Place 1 claim marker on a sector you occupy (your units are there) that has no owner
and is claimable. Claim your **3rd, 6th, 9th…** sector: **gain 1 morale.**
*Claiming is your long game: claims give Fuel income and points.*

### HARVEST (free)
If your units occupy a sector with a terrain bonus and no Harvest token:
take the bonus and put a Harvest token there. (Until end of round.)
| Terrain | Bonus |
|---------|-------|
| Barren | 1 Alloy |
| Hostile | 1 Fuel |
| Asteroid Field | 2 Alloy |
| Gas Giant | 2 Fuel |
| Ruins | 1 Data |

### BUILD (spend Alloy, +Data for the Choir)
Build in a sector you control (you own its claim or colony):
| Unit | Cost | Limit | Effect |
|------|------|-------|--------|
| **Frigate** | 2 Alloy (Sorvath: 1) | 12 (Concord: 11) | Your muscle. Fleet and battle power. |
| **Colony Dome** | 4 Alloy (Ashkari: 5) | 5 | Needs a colony-capable sector you control (Terrestrial — Ellarien can also colonize Barren/Hostile at Tier 1). **+2 morale** (+3 Ellarien off-home). 3 points each at the end! |
| **Outpost Beacon** | 1 Alloy | 6 | +2 strength when **defending** its sector. |

*The Choir pays 1 Data extra on every build. Flesh is weak; their budgets are weaker.*

### DRAW (cost: 2 Data)
Draw 1 Tactical Doctrine card (hand limit: 3).

## 7. Income (every round, at Upkeep)

| You have… | You gain… |
|-----------|-----------|
| Your home sector | 1 Alloy + 1 Fuel |
| Each Colony Dome | 2 Alloy |
| Each claimed sector | 1 Fuel |

*The Choir also gains +1 Data each round, from the archive tithe. Volkrath gains 1
less resource overall — glory doesn't smelt.*

## 8. Battles

When you MOVE into a sector with enemy Frigates:

1. **Choose posture.** Each side secretly picks 1 Tactics card from hand — or none.
2. **Reveal** together.
3. **Roll** 1 die each.
4. **Add your strength:**
   - 1 per own Frigate in the sector
   - Defender with an Outpost there: **+2**
   - Defending Ellarien: **+1** (Roots of the Verdant)
   - Card value
   - Die roll
   - House modifiers (House sheet): e.g. Volkrath +1 at Tier 1
5. **High total wins.** Ties go to the **defender** (unless Volkrath's Terror Advance
   is active — then Volkrath wins ties).
6. **Aftermath:**
   - The **loser retreats** their whole fleet to an adjacent empty or own sector.
     Nowhere to go? The fleet is destroyed. (Feint: the loser skips the morale loss.)
   - Loser: **−1 morale** (Volkrath: −2; Overwhelm loser: −1 extra). Winner: **+1 morale.**
   - If the **attacker** won: take over the sector — replace the loser's claim marker
     with yours (free). If they had a Colony there, it's **yours now** (the previous
     owner: −1 morale). Transfer impossible? Remove it.
   - Sacrifice card: its player removes 1 own Frigate from the sector.

**Worked example.** Volkrath (attacker) moves 3 Frigates into Ellarien's sector
(Ellarien has 2 Frigates + an Outpost).
- Volkrath plays no card, rolls 4: strength = 3 + 0 + 4 = **7** (+1 Warborn at Tier 1 → **8**)
- Ellarien plays *Fortify* (+3), rolls 2: strength = 2 + 2 (Outpost) + 1 (Roots of
  the Verdant) + 3 + 2 = **10**
- **Ellarien holds!** Volkrath retreats, −1 morale. Ellarien +1 morale.
  (Without that Fortify, the Machine-Garden would have fallen at 8 vs 7.)

## 9. Morale — the Heart of Your House

Your Morale dial (0–10) is your House's spirit. Move it:
| Event | Change |
|-------|--------|
| Win a battle | +1 |
| Build a Colony | +2 |
| 3rd/6th/9th… claim | +1 |
| Bold dilemma choice | +1 |
| Lose a battle | −1 |
| Colony captured | −1 |
| Overextension at round end | −1 |

**Tier 1 (morale 6+):** your House's first power switches on (House sheet).
**Tier 2 (morale 8+):** your second power ignites.
**Last Breath (morale 2 or less):** at the start of your turn, draw 1 Tactics card.
Desperation is a teacher.

**Initiative reminder:** the House with the LOWEST morale acts first each round.
Desperation moves first. Pride waits its turn.

---

## 10. Drift (Events)

From round 2, flip 1 Drift card each round. Events hit everyone: alloy windfalls,
plague decks, refugee barges, ghost convoys. When a **refugee** card asks you to
choose, you choose: take the hope (+1 morale, pay 1 Alloy) or take the cargo
(+1 Alloy, −1 morale). When the deck runs out, reshuffle it.

---

## 11. Dilemmas — the Weight of Survival

When the doom track hits a dilemma icon, each player takes the top card of their
House's dilemma deck and reads it aloud. Every dilemma has two answers:

- **The pragmatic choice:** a small material gain. You keep the metal.
- **The bold choice:** a small cost, **+1 morale**, and **1 extra point at game end.**
  It is who your House chooses to be.

Keep resolved dilemmas face-up in front of you — count your bold points at the end.
When a deck runs out, no more dilemmas. The House has become what it is.

**Example — "The Gardens" (House Sorvath).** *Decommission the hydroponic gardens
for alloy?* **Pragmatic:** +4 Alloy. **Bold:** keep them growing: −1 Fuel, +1 morale,
+1 point. Your call, Commander.

---

## 12. Earth's Last Breath

The doom dial tracks Earth's dying: **1 segment per round for rounds 1–4, then 2
per round**, on a track of 16 segments. When the track ends, the game ends
immediately — final scoring (§3). Dilemma icons sit on every 3rd segment. Some
Drift events push Earth faster. You cannot save Earth. You can decide what it *meant*.

---

## 13. Exodus — your secret mission

Your Exodus card tells you exactly what to hold when a round ends:
- **The Lifeboat:** 3 colonies + 4 claims.
- **The Long Watch:** 2 colonies on wormhole sectors + morale 8+.
- **The Inheritance:** 3 Ruins sectors + 2 colonies.
Fulfilled at the end of a round = **instant win**, doom track be damned.

## 16. Quick Reference

**Turn:** 2 Actions — Move (1 Fuel) · Claim (1 Fuel) · Harvest · Build · Draw (2 Data).
**Battle:** card + die + frigates + bonus. High wins. Loser retreats.
**Morale:** battle win +1 / colony +2 / milestone claim +1 / bold choice +1 /
loss −1 / capture −1 / overextension −1.
**Tiers:** 6 = Tier 1 power. 8 = Tier 2 power. ≤2 = Last Breath draw.
**End:** doom track done → score. Exodus done → win now.

*Earth is counting on no one. Good luck, Commander.*
